# Turkish community TTS efforts (2023 – October 2026): who trained/fine-tuned what, on which data, how, and with what results

Research date: 2026-10-26. Hugging Face download/like counts were read from the HF API on that date (downloads = HF's rolling 30-day figure). Many 2026 releases are only weeks old, so counts are low even for well-regarded models. X/Twitter post bodies could not be fetched directly (HTTP 402); the quoted X text below comes from search-result snippets and is marked as such.

A shared benchmark emerged in July 2026 and is now used by almost every Turkish release: **Freya-TR-Eval** (495 conversational Turkish sentences, WER/CER via Whisper large-v3 after 8 kHz band-matching, UTMOS22 for naturalness). Numbers from different cards are *not* strictly comparable (different seeds, normalisers, harnesses); the PatientDesk re-run (7 Oct 2026) is the only single-harness leaderboard across ~20 systems.

---

## KQ1. Which Turkish TTS fine-tunes / trainings exist on Hugging Face (by base model), with authors, dates, data, recipe, results, and openness?

### Takeaway
Between March 2025 and October 2026 Turkish developers published well over 40 Turkish TTS checkpoints on Hugging Face. The 2025 wave was mostly Orpheus (SNAC-LLM) and F5-TTS fine-tunes on Common Voice or YouTube-narrator data with little documentation; the mid/late-2026 wave is dominated by (a) VoxCPM2 LoRAs (Trendyol-TTS, Ahenk, mramazan), (b) Qwen3-TTS adaptations (erkamk), (c) tiny from-scratch single-voice models trained on *synthetic* VoxCPM2 speech (EMA Lightning 8.6M, Antalia-2 Mini 7.6M, Ahenk Cep), and (d) well-documented LoRAs on IndexTTS-2.5, Chatterbox, Pocket TTS and Drama 3. The best-documented cards (EMA, Antalia, IndexTTS-2.5-TR, AKIL, erkamk, Kur0Keri, AbDhumal) give full recipes; most 2025 Orpheus/F5 cards give none.

### Cited Findings

**VoxCPM2-based (OpenBMB, Apache-2.0 base, 2B params)**
- **Trendyol/Trendyol-TTS** (Trendyol, Turkish e-commerce company): created 2026-06-03, MIT, 705 downloads / 45 likes (26 Oct 2026). "Turkish text-to-speech research model built on top of openbmb/VoxCPM2 … based on the Trendyol Turkish TTS LoRA training run and uses the manually selected `step_0002000` checkpoint"; merged weights plus the original LoRA adapter are shipped; "Fine-tuned for Turkish speech generation using a private Turkish voice dataset"; recommended inference `cfg_value=2.0`, `inference_timesteps=16`. — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/Trendyol/Trendyol-TTS)
- Trendyol's training data is not described on the card; the third-party `turkish-tts` PyPI package says the weights "were already fine-tuned for general Turkish using more than 20 hours of proprietary data, referenced in a `merge_manifest.json`" (unverified beyond that manifest). — [PyPI turkish-tts](https://pypi.org/project/turkish-tts/); [Boran-Sert/Turkish-TTS](https://github.com/Boran-Sert/Turkish-TTS)
- Trendyol-TTS on the PatientDesk re-run: 2.38B params, WER 1.16 ± 0.16 %, CER 0.23 %, 93.1 % clean sentences, UTMOS 3.82 (the highest UTMOS of all 20 systems), RTF 0.962 on an L4. — [PatientdeskAI/freya-tr-eval-rerun](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PatientdeskAI/freya-tr-eval-rerun)
- Ecosystem around Trendyol-TTS: `emrekoc/trendyol-tts-mlx` (2026-06-11, Apple-Silicon MLX port, text-only, no cloning/streaming yet) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/emrekoc/trendyol-tts-mlx); `turkish-tts` 1.0.0 on PyPI (Boran Sert, 2026-10-09, Apache-2.0) wraps the weights with streaming, HTTP/WebSocket server, voice cloning and a `TurkishTTS.finetune()` LoRA workflow whose defaults "match Trendyol's values … 2000 steps and LoRA rank 64". — [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/turkish-tts/)
- **ozgurguler/ahenk-tts-tr "Ahenk"** (HF `ozgurguler`, GitHub `ozguradmin`): created 2026-10-16, CC-BY-4.0, 21 likes. "VoxCPM2 için Türkçe bir LoRA adaptörüdür: 2B taban model + 36.2 M parametrelik LoRA, 48 kHz, tek bir tasarlanmış kadın sesi (f022), 6 duygu ve gülme"; the voice was *designed* in VoxCPM2 from an English text description ("gerçek bir kişinin sesi … değildir"); trained on PatientDesk's synthetic Alania corpus (stage 1) and Alania domain text (stage 2) plus the author's own question/emotion/laughter texts; released checkpoint `voxcpm2-f022-s3tr/step_0000500`, 16 steps, cfg 2.0, with the `trnorm` text front-end. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/ozgurguler/ahenk-tts-tr)
- Ahenk's own benchmark "TR-Zor" (155 hard sentences with numbers, dates, times, money, abbreviations, phone numbers): Ahenk WER 2.54 % (141/155 error-free) vs Trendyol-TTS 3.98 % (134/155); on 20 held-out long sentences Trendyol wins (0.68 % vs 0.82 %) and has higher UTMOS (3.82/3.73 vs 3.70/3.14); Ahenk RTF 2.28 on an A100 80 GB. Known issues: yes/no questions written without "mı" end like statements; breath sounds remain in pauses (`pause_clean` mitigates); some emotional clips "bright or crackly". — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/ozgurguler/ahenk-tts-tr); [GitHub ozguradmin/ahenk-turkish-tts](https://github.com/ozguradmin/ahenk-turkish-tts)
- **ozgurguler/ahenk-cep-tr**: 2026-10-16, CC-BY-4.0, "8.6M parametre, 34 MB (fp32), EMA Lightning mimarisi" student distilled from Ahenk's voice, RTF ~0.2 on 2 CPU threads, browser demo at ozguradmin.github.io; TR-Zor WER 2.32 %. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/ozgurguler/ahenk-cep-tr)
- **mramazan/voxcpm2-turkish-tts-lora**: created 2026-06-30, Apache-2.0, "Turkish LoRA adapter … using ~18 hours of single-speaker data", LoRA rank 64 / alpha 64, checkpoint step 1400, preprocessing "Turkish-specific number/date normalization (`trnorm`), -23 LUFS loudness normalization, sentence integrity filtering, 3–20 second utterance range"; "Stability issues may occur with very short (<3 seconds) or highly expressive input texts." — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/mramazan/voxcpm2-turkish-tts-lora)
- **alicangunduz/nefes-0.5 "Nefes"** (Space by hugging-apps, 2026-10-22, Apache-2.0): "training-free, fully open expressive TTS system for Turkish" chaining VoxCPM2 continuations with inline tags `[gül]`, `[iç çek]`, `[fısılda]`, reranking 6 candidates by WavLM-SV similarity, Whisper WER and a small human-preference model (~30 s per 6 s sentence). — [HF Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hugging-apps/nefes-expressive-turkish-tts)
- **Vyvo/drifting-tts-tr-voxcpm2** (2026-10-08, experimental): one-step drifting model generating VoxCPM2 AudioVAE latents (64 ch, 25 Hz), same recipe/data as drifting-tts v3.1. — [HF](https://huggingface.co/Vyvo/drifting-tts-tr-voxcpm2)

**Tiny from-scratch single-voice models trained on synthetic VoxCPM2 speech (the Oct-2026 "8M-parameter" wave)**
- **canberkkkkkk/ema-lightning "EMA Lightning"**: created 2026-10-01, Apache-2.0, **63,008 downloads / 447 likes** (by far the most-downloaded Turkish-specific TTS on HF). "A 5.6M-parameter DiT acoustic model and a 3M-parameter vocoder: 8.6M in total, about 34 MB"; architecture: per-letter text encoder → duration predictor → windowed Gaussian aligner → 4-step DiT generating 64-dim latents at 25 Hz → HiFi-GAN-style decoder to 48 kHz; text normalised with `normalizer-tr` (by Erdem Tuna). Training data: "An internal corpus of about 1,000 hours of single-speaker Turkish speech, all synthetic, generated with VoxCPM2 … The voice is not a real person's: it was designed in VoxCPM2 from a short text description". Self-reported Freya-TR-Eval: WER 0.92 ± 0.13 %, CER 0.18 %, 440× real time (RTX 4090, compiled), 1,316× batched; UTMOS ≈ 3.30; limitations: one voice, no cloning, no emotion, Turkish only. GitHub user `canberk7` per a forum post. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/canberkkkkkk/ema-lightning); [r10.net thread](https://www.r10.net/yapay-zeka/4896586-ema-lightning-acik-kaynakli-ucretsiz-bir-turkce-tts-modeli.html)
- EMA's card does not state GPU, steps, batch size or learning rate for the acoustic model (gap). The card's own benchmark table lists Trendyol-TTS at 0.98 %, Anka 1.73 %, Chatterbox MTL 1.84 %, Piper 3.22 %, XTTS-v2 3.34 %, MMS-TTS 6.41 %, FreyaTTS 12.02 %, F5 base (no Turkish) 77.84 %. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/canberkkkkkk/ema-lightning)
- **cloud0day3/antalia-mini "Antalia-2 Mini"** (PatientDesk AI): created 2026-10-08, Apache-2.0 (code, normaliser and weights), 5,807 downloads / 55 likes. "7.62M parameters in total (acoustic model 3.69M, vocoder 3.93M), produces 48 kHz audio … runs well faster than real time on a CPU"; ships its own Turkish normaliser (numbers, dates, times, amounts, phone numbers, URLs, initialisms, English/brand respelling via CMU dict); browser demo via ONNX Runtime Web. Training data: "About 1,578 hours of synthetic speech, all in Antalia-2's voice, generated with VoxCPM2 … text came from Turkish Wikipedia, FineWeb-2 Turkish, customer-service style sentences written with Qwen3, templates for numbers/dates/times/amounts/phone numbers"; every clip auto-checked by Whisper, UTMOS and voice similarity. Self-reported Freya-TR-Eval WER 1.02 % (1.0.1) / 0.97 % (1.0.0), CER 0.21 %, UTMOS 3.18; the VoxCPM2 teacher voice itself scored 0.92 %. Limitations: one voice; "Short interjections on their own ('Evet.', 'Hı hı.') are its weakest area"; a stray "l" before "ö" at the very start of text learned from the training audio; prosody does not carry across ~18 s chunks. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/cloud0day3/antalia-mini)
- Antalia-2 Mini was covered by AlphaSignal as "Antalia-2 Mini Beats 2.38B Models on Turkish Speech at Just 30MB". — [alphasignal.ai](https://alphasignal.ai/news/antalia-2-mini-beats-2-38b-models-on-turkish-speech-at-just-30mb)

**Qwen3-TTS-based (Alibaba, Apache-2.0)**
- **erkamk/qwen3-0.6b-tr "Qwen3 0.6B Turkish"**: created 2026-09-16, Apache-2.0, 672 downloads. Full adaptation of `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base` "trained on roughly 2,277 hours of Turkish read speech from Kiraat" (1,388,236 clips, 54 speakers, 2,540 source recordings), "one epoch (21,691 updates) with 64 clips per update. Dev loss went from 2.885 to 2.710"; zero-shot voice cloning from a 5–10 s reference. Limitations quoted: "Numbers written as digits … `1.299,90 TL` is often garbled"; "`WhatsApp`, `iPhone`, and mixed Turkish-English sentences come out unstable. Code-switching is not supported"; "under-trained rather than converged". — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/erkamk/qwen3-0.6b-tr)
- **erkamk/qwen3-tts-tiny-tr**: created 2026-10-02, Apache-2.0, 533 downloads / 12 likes. 199M-parameter (4.6× smaller) Qwen3-TTS structure trained on Alania synthetic speech (1,451,721 clips, ~1,863 h, 2,728 synthetic voices): "SFT: 90,000 steps of 64 clips" then "RL: group-relative policy optimisation on the whole talker, 253 steps of 16 sentences × 8 samples" with WavLM speaker-similarity + UTMOS22 rewards and a KL penalty. Freya-TR-Eval (495): WER 1.28 %, CER 0.42 % vs Qwen3 0.6B Turkish 1.4 % / 0.29 %; on 120 sentences with unseen *real* voices: WER 3.3 %, speaker-sim 0.46, UTMOS 3.58 (0.6B model: 2.0 %, 0.39, 3.76). RTF 1.08 on an L4; runs faster than real time on an Apple M5 CPU. Known issues: slower speech after RL; inputs >12 s "can end early and drop the last words". — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/erkamk/qwen3-tts-tiny-tr)
- **hcfk/qwen3-tts-turkish** (experimental LoRA, created 2026-06-13): "can synthesize understandable Turkish speech, but it may retain a foreign accent due to the base model's acoustic priors"; FINDINGS.md: attention-only rank-64 LoRA on `q/k/v/o_proj` "hits a ceiling on accent reduction" (hypothesis: Turkish phoneme-to-codec mappings live in FFN layers); the code-predictor "sub loss" is not a perceptual proxy; training the code predictor jointly degraded audio ("`cp_lr = 0` in all … runs"); best result from "lr = 1e-7, cp frozen, 500 steps"; data directory named `issai_tokens` (i.e. ISSAI Turkish Speech Corpus); advises writing numbers/dates/abbreviations as words. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/hcfk/qwen3-tts-turkish); [FINDINGS.md](https://huggingface.co/hcfk/qwen3-tts-turkish/raw/main/FINDINGS.md)

**Orpheus-TTS (Canopy Labs, Llama-3B + SNAC) – the 2025 wave**
- **Karayakar/Orpheus-TTS-Turkish-PT-5000** (Karay Akar, LinkedIn `karayakar`): created 2025-04-16, MIT, 67 downloads / 10 likes; "trained based on canopylabs/orpheus-3b-0.1-pretrained. Syntethic voice data over 60 hrs used for initial training. +160hrs additional Syntethic voice data mixed in training. 400 Emoji (real voice) data used for emoji support"; supports `<laugh> <chuckle> <sigh> <cough> <sniffle> <groan> <yawn> <gasp>` tags; Flask API and `train.py`/`config.yaml` included; GGUF variants (PT-2000, PT-5000-Q5_K_M) also published; demo Space `Karayakar/Orpheus-TTS-Turkish` (13 likes, created 2025-04-15). — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/Karayakar/Orpheus-TTS-Turkish-PT-5000); [Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Karayakar/Orpheus-TTS-Turkish)
- **Cosmobillian/turkish_orpheus_tts** (2025-05-10, Apache-2.0): Unsloth/TRL fine-tune *of* Karayakar's PT-5000 (no data details). — [HF](https://huggingface.co/Cosmobillian/turkish_orpheus_tts)
- **kadirnar/orpheus-tts-tr-full** (2025-03-22) and **kadirnar/Orpheus-TTS-TR-Beta** (2025-04-06), **kadirnar/Orpheus-TTS-Youtube-TR** (2025-04-10): Unsloth fine-tunes of `canopylabs/orpheus-3b-0.1-pretrained`; the first is tagged with dataset `erenfazlioglu/turkishvoicedataset`; cards are empty templates. — [HF](https://huggingface.co/kadirnar/orpheus-tts-tr-full)
- **AquaLabs/Orpheus-3B-0.1-ft-Common-Voice-Turkish** (March 2025; now returns "Invalid username or password", i.e. private/removed) survives as `mradermacher/Orpheus-3B-0.1-ft-Common-Voice-Turkish-GGUF` (2025-03-30, Apache-2.0). — [HF GGUF](https://huggingface.co/mradermacher/Orpheus-3B-0.1-ft-Common-Voice-Turkish-GGUF)
- **omersaidd/tts_mazlum_kiper_tur_001** (2025-07-24, MIT, 74 downloads): fine-tune of `canopylabs/orpheus-3b-0.1-ft` on the author's own YouTube-narrator dataset `omersaidd/tts_mazlum_kiper_tur`; "Amacım Türkçe tarafında kaliteli veri setleri oluşturmak ve açık kaynak TTS modellerin Türkçe performansını artırabilmektir"; research use only, rights stay with the narrator Mazlum Kiper. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/omersaidd/tts_mazlum_kiper_tur_001)
- **AbDhumal/orpheus-3b-turkish-tts-v2** (2026-06-11, Apache-2.0; author is a Red Hat engineer, not Turkish): LoRA r=32/α=64/dropout 0.05 on q/k/v/o + gate/up/down (~185M trainable) of `unsloth/orpheus-3b-0.1-pretrained`; data = 20,000 of the ~81k samples in `afkfatih/turkish-tts-combined-raw`, SNAC 24 kHz, max 4,096 tokens, 95/5 split; LR 2e-5, 8 epochs, 9,504 steps, effective batch 16 (2 nodes × A100 80 GB, per-device 2 × accum 4), warmup 0.1, bf16 + FlashAttention-2, best eval_loss at step 8,800 (9.50 → 4.35); 10-sentence Whisper-small benchmark WER 1.576 → 0.723; use case "airline-style Turkish announcements". — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/AbDhumal/orpheus-3b-turkish-tts-v2)
- Other Orpheus-TR artefacts without documentation: `vsqrd/orpheus-turkish-finetune-v1` (2026-03-27, PEFT adapter), `vsqrd/orpheus-3b-turkish-pretrain` (2026-05-10), `TurkishCodeMan/orpheus-3b-0.1-ft-lora-fft` (2025-10-01), dataset `mukahraman/orpheus-tts-turkish-mozilla17` (2025-10-21, Common Voice 17 tokenised for Orpheus). — [HF search results](https://huggingface.co/models?search=orpheus%20turkish)

**IndexTTS-2.5, Chatterbox, Pocket TTS, Drama 3, Kani, Dia, CSM, Llasa, OuteTTS (2026 LoRAs/fine-tunes)**
- **berrkkaayy/IndexTTS-2.5-Turkish-LoRA** (v1 2026-08-19, v2 2026-10-08, bilibili licence): adds Turkish to IndexTTS-2.5 keeping zh/en/ja/es/ar; data "486.5 h Turkish, 143,609 utterances, 3,652 speakers; 286,606 prompt/target pairs"; LoRA r32/α64 on all GPT blocks, `lang_embedding` row 9 seeded from `es`; "LR 1e-4, bf16, effective batch 32, 13,000 steps (~1.45 epochs) on an RTX 5070 Ti + RTX 4070"; **v1 → v2 WER 12.36 % → 1.32 %** by training "1,530 untrained text-embedding rows" that "carry 37% of the Turkish token occurrences" (v1 "swapped short Turkish words for other words (*yağmur → 'arkadaş mur'*)"); UTMOS 3.17; RTF 0.83 on an RTX 5070 Ti; trainer at github.com/oberkayb/index-tts-2.5-ft. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/berrkkaayy/IndexTTS-2.5-Turkish-LoRA)
- **Ertugrul1611/akil-tts-tr "AKIL TTS 1.0"** (Ertuğrul Akkuş, AKIL AI; eval dataset created 2026-10-17, CC-BY-4.0): Chatterbox Multilingual V2 + LoRA "r = 32 and α = 64 on the q/k/v/o and gate/up/down projections of all 30 T3 layers", loss = shifted CE on speech tokens + 0.1 × text loss, 10 % text dropout for CFG, "5,000 steps, batch 16"; data = YODAS2 Turkish (`tr000` manual subtitles shards 0–29 + every 17th `tr100` shard), 31,207 clips from 1,647 videos, clips 2–14 s kept when Whisper CER ≤ 0.10; 60-sentence × 9-voice frozen eval set with 4,020 scored clips; limitations: words "occasionally omitted or cut, mostly in very short sentences", "Digits must be normalized". — [HF eval dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Ertugrul1611/akil-tts-tr-eval)
- **wite-tech/pocket-tts-turkish** (2026-09-28, CC-BY-4.0, 7 likes): 6-layer Pocket TTS (Kyutai) fine-tune, "About 110 million parameters (codec included), 440 MB download, 24 kHz", "About 5 times faster than real time on one CPU thread", six synthetic designed voices, cloning from a few seconds, emotion tags `mutlu/üzgün/kızgın/şaşkın/sakin`; PyPI/GitHub `witetech/pocket-tts-turkish`; Core ML/ANE port `TheStageAI/pocket-tts-100m-tr` (2026-10-24). - **alperiox/pocket-tts-turkish** (2026-10-11, MIT): 24-layer teacher fine-tuned from `english_2026-04_24l` for 30k steps on `alperiox/alania-tr-250h-24k` (262.5 h, 2,647 synthetic voices, 50/50 gender), then depth-distilled to 6 layers for 160k steps; "one RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. ~1.5 h teacher, ~4.4 h student"; student WER 11.88 % = the ASR's own error on ground truth, UTMOS 3.733, SIM-o 0.955. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/alperiox/pocket-tts-turkish)

- **kasparovabi/drama-3-tr-speaker** (2026-10-20): a distilled Turkish "speaker" for Fish Audio's 35B-MoE Drama 3 keeping 32 of 256 experts per layer, running on an RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB with experts streamed from NVMe; "Training took about 85 minutes on 3,800 utterances from the ISSAI Turkish Speech Corpus" using a GPTAQ-style trajectory-anchoring objective (KL 0.109 vs 0.153 for function matching); Freya-TR-Eval WER 2.27–2.78 %, CER 0.62–0.70 %; post-release bug: switching to guided generation reallocated the KV cache and silently dropped the reference voice ("The model was speaking with no reference voice at all"). ### Inferences
- The 2025 Orpheus/F5 wave mostly used Common Voice 17 or scraped YouTube narrators and produced few usable results; the 2026 wave shifted to VoxCPM2 (which already speaks Turkish well, WER ~1.65 % on its internal benchmark per the base README) either as a LoRA target or as a *teacher* generating thousands of hours of synthetic single-voice data for tiny models. This is why three different 8M-parameter Turkish models appeared within three weeks of each other (EMA, Antalia-2 Mini, Ahenk Cep).
- Models trained on synthetic VoxCPM2 speech reach the lowest ASR-WER on Freya-TR-Eval but mid-range UTMOS (3.1–3.3); Trendyol-TTS (real private data, 2B) has the best naturalness proxy. For an *expressive* target, the LoRA-on-large-model route (VoxCPM2/IndexTTS-2.5/Drama 3) is where emotion control currently lives.
- The IndexTTS-2.5-TR v1→v2 result (12.36 % → 1.32 %) and the SpeechT5 tokenizer finding together indicate that untrained text-embedding rows for Turkish graphemes/sub-words are the single biggest silent failure mode when adapting English/Chinese-centric bases.

### Gaps
- Trendyol's actual dataset (source, hours, speakers), GPU and LR are not published; only "step_0002000" and the "20+ hours proprietary" note from a third-party manifest are known.
- EMA Lightning's and Anka TTS's training hyper-parameters (GPU, steps, batch, LR) are absent from their cards; Anka's training data is only "Turkish speech data".
- Several HF repos have no card at all (mehmetalierol2 XTTS, vsqrd Orpheus, alpcansoydas MOSS-TTS-Nano, kadirnar CSM, 99eren99 Piper training details); AquaLabs' Orpheus CV-Turkish and `seslen-tr/seslen-tts` HF repos are now private or gated.
- No MOS/listening study exists for any community model; all quality numbers are Whisper-WER and UTMOS (English-trained) proxies, which several authors themselves flag as unreliable for Turkish.

Fine-tuning hyper-parameters are not reported. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06057v1); [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06057)
- **ISSAI (Nazarbayev University, Kazakhstan, not Turkey)** maintains the Turkish Speech Corpus (218.2 h, 186,171 utterances; MIT) and TurkicTTS, both still used by Turkish developers (hcfk, kasparovabi). — [issai/Turkish_Speech_Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/issai/Turkish_Speech_Corpus)
- The only earlier Turkish-authored open TTS recipe still referenced is Rumeysa Keskin's FastPitch-from-scratch + HiFi-GAN fine-tune repo (2023), plus her "free-turkish-tts-models" comparison (XTTS-v2 rated 5/5 "Native", MMS-TTS 4/5). — [Rumeysakeskin/Turkish-Text-to-Speech](https://github.com/Rumeysakeskin/Turkish-Text-to-Speech); [free-turkish-tts-models](https://github.com/Rumeysakeskin/free-turkish-tts-models)

### Inferences

### Takeaway
Real-speech sources cluster into (1) Common Voice 17/26 Turkish (~130 validated h), (2) YouTube-derived corpora — single narrators (Mazlum Kiper, Ahmet Deniz, Nisan Kumru, BirDinle, Sesli Fikir İstasyonu), Khan Academy TR (78 h), YODAS2 (AKIL), Turkish podcasts (yt-data-1, Vyvo/tr-dataset-12 93 h), and Serdar Çağlar's gated audiobook corpora (turkish-tts-audiobooks 2,724 h; Kiraat ~2,277 h, 54 speakers), (3) ISSAI TSC (218 h) and FLEURS, (4) one consented studio voice (Antalia, 5 h). ## KQ6 (cross-cutting). Training recipes, reported quality, and Turkish-specific problems people hit

### Takeaway
Across cards the recurring Turkish problems are: digits/dates/amounts (every model wants spoken-form text; `trnorm`, `normalizer-tr`, `turkce-normalize`, `normalize_tr.py` were all written to fix this), untrained grapheme/sub-word rows for ç ğ ı İ ö ş ü (SpeechT5, IndexTTS-2.5), foreign accent ceilings when LoRA-adapting Chinese/English bases (Qwen3-TTS 0.6B/1.7B), code-switching with English brand names, mı/mi question intonation, long-input duration under-budgeting, and ASR-based metrics confounded by Whisper's own Turkish errors.

### Cited Findings
- Recipes with full hyper-parameters: AbDhumal Orpheus (LoRA r32/α64, LR 2e-5, batch 16, 9,504 steps, 2×A100), IndexTTS-2.5-TR (LoRA r32/α64, LR 1e-4, batch 32, 13k steps, RTX 5070 Ti + 4070), AKIL Chatterbox (LoRA r32/α64, 5,000 steps, batch 16), omersaidd CSM (full FT, LR 2e-4, 4,000 steps, A100, 4 h), Kur0Keri VITS (batch 16, LR 2e-4 → 1e-4, 157,827 + 22,950 steps, RTX 5060 8 GB), erkamk Qwen3 0.6B (1 epoch, 21,691 updates × 64 clips) and tiny (SFT 90k × 64 + GRPO 253 steps), alperiox Pocket (30k teacher + 160k distill steps, RTX PRO 6000, ~6 h), drifting-tts (150k steps, 13.6 h, RTX 5090), DACVAE (60k updates, 9.5 h, RTX 4090), mramazan VoxCPM2 (LoRA r64, step 1400), Werea (lr 2e-6, batch 8, step 75), Antalia-1 (100k foundation + 18.5k staged updates). — see KQ1 citations above
- Tokenizer/embedding failures: SpeechT5's 79-piece English SentencePiece lacks all Turkish letters and digits (thealper2); IndexTTS-2.5's 1,530 never-trained text-embedding rows carried 37 % of Turkish token occurrences and caused "yağmur → arkadaş mur" (berrkkaayy). — [thealper2](https://huggingface.co/thealper2/speecht5-tts-turkish); [berrkkaayy](https://huggingface.co/berrkkaayy/IndexTTS-2.5-Turkish-LoRA)
- Accent ceiling on Qwen3-TTS: hcfk ("retains a perceptible foreign accent … Attention-only LoRA (rank 64) hits a ceiling") and Alican Kiraz ("aksan konusu hala zorluyor"). Full adaptation on 2,277 h (erkamk) removes the accent but is "under-trained" after one epoch. — [hcfk FINDINGS](https://huggingface.co/hcfk/qwen3-tts-turkish/raw/main/FINDINGS.md); [AlicanKiraz0](https://x.com/AlicanKiraz0/status/2050702869280563691); [erkamk](https://huggingface.co/erkamk/qwen3-0.6b-tr)
- Numbers/abbreviations: Orkhon, Kur0Keri, Antalia-1, hcfk, erkamk, AKIL and IndexTTS-TR all say digits must be verbalised; Antalia-1: "Numbers, normalization-heavy text, foreign names and abbreviations have the highest error rates"; Antalia-2 Mini and EMA ship normalisers; Ahenk's TR-Zor benchmark exists specifically for this. — [Antalia-1](https://huggingface.co/cloud0day3/antalia-1); [Ahenk](https://huggingface.co/ozgurguler/ahenk-tts-tr)
- Prosody/questions: Ahenk — questions without "mı" "end like statements"; seslen-tts adds "rising pitch on the stressed syllable before the question suffix" and syllable stress via Turkish PL-BERT; drifting-tts v3.2 added a stochastic pitch model because v3.1's intonation was "the same flat average". — [Ahenk GitHub](https://github.com/ozguradmin/ahenk-turkish-tts); [seslen](https://github.com/seslen-tr/seslen-tts); [drifting](https://huggingface.co/Vyvo/drifting-tts-tr)
- Code-switching: erkamk — "`WhatsApp`, `iPhone`, and mixed Turkish-English sentences come out unstable"; EMA/Antalia/Kur0Keri read foreign words with Turkish letter rules; Antalia-2 Mini respells English/brand words via the CMU dictionary. — [erkamk](https://huggingface.co/erkamk/qwen3-0.6b-tr); [antalia-mini](https://huggingface.co/cloud0day3/antalia-mini)
- Long inputs: Antalia-1 under-budgets duration (chunk ≤120 chars, 160 ms pauses); erkamk tiny drops last words beyond ~12 s; Antalia-2 Mini and IndexTTS-TR note prosody resets between chunks. — [Antalia-1](https://huggingface.co/cloud0day3/antalia-1); [erkamk tiny](https://huggingface.co/erkamk/qwen3-tts-tiny-tr)
- Metric caveats raised by authors: Whisper counts spoken numbers written as digits as errors (erkamk, seslen); UTMOS is English-trained (Ahenk, AKIL, Kur0Keri, re-run); Freya-TR-Eval was designed to neutralise "Whisper's own Turkish error rate, a narrowband penalty against telephony-band systems, and digit-normalization artifacts". — [freya-tr-eval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/freyavoice/freya-tr-eval)
- Data-quality lessons: marduk-ra — 4 h is too little for F5 fine-tuning, "dozens of hours" needed; serdarildercaglar — Whisper's `''` quotes vs suffix apostrophes, ~25 % mid-sentence fragments; DACVAE — in-domain fine-tune improved the training monitor but regressed on the held-out benchmark; hcfk — codec "sub loss" is not a perceptual proxy. — [marduk-ra discussion](https://huggingface.co/marduk-ra/F5-TTS-Turkish/discussions/2); [serdarildercaglar](https://github.com/serdarildercaglar/turkish-tts-model); [dacvae](https://huggingface.co/VoiceHub/dacvae-tts-tr-w512)

### Inferences
- For an expressive Turkish voice from TV/audiobook data, the community evidence favours LoRA on VoxCPM2 (rank 64, ~1–2k steps, 16-step/cfg 2.0 inference) or IndexTTS-2.5 (with text-row training), with a spoken-form normaliser (`trnorm`/`normalizer-tr`) in the data pipeline and Freya-TR-Eval + a hard-sentence set (TR-Zor-style) for checkpoint selection.

### Gaps
- No community report quantifies ğ-specific or vowel-harmony-specific error rates; these appear only as motivation in blog posts, not as measured failure modes.
- No listener MOS study exists for any of the open Turkish models; the only human evaluation is Freya's 7-listener test (unpublished details) and Antalia-1's single-listener session.
